MedDraft

Spoken clinical notes, turned into hospital documents that a doctor reviews and approves.

Read the full case

Simulation: how a draft shows its sourcesInvented patient. Not a MedDraft screen.

What the doctor said

पेशंटला दोन दिवसांपासून ताप आहे, cough भी है. BP one-thirty over eighty. Paracetamol 500, दिवसातून तीन वेळा, five days.

What the form holds

Draft. Waiting for a doctor.

Approved

Point at a field, or tab to it, to see the words behind it. The dictation mixes Marathi, Hindi and English on purpose. Roughly: “The patient has had a fever for two days, and a cough. BP 130 over 80. Paracetamol 500, three times a day, five days.”

noticed

A doctor's notes are spoken long before they are typed, often in Marathi, Hindi and English inside one sentence. The hospital's forms want fixed fields.

thought through

Software can fill the form, but a doctor has to own it. So every value points back to the words it came from, and nothing is final until a doctor approves it.

built

A documentation tool for Gurukrupa Hospital. Dictate, get a structured draft, check the gaps it flags, approve, then export to Word or PDF in the hospital's own formats.

now

Built and documented.

Not measured yet. Time saved, accuracy, and how widely it is used.

Kuchu Puchu

Small desktop characters that carry a hello, a hug or a film night to a friend far away.

Read the full case

A friend's desktop, somewhere far awaySketch of the idea

Press a gesture. This little one is my own drawing for this page. The real characters, Sparks and the Couch are at kuchupuchu.me.

noticed

Keeping up with friends in other cities mostly happens in a chat window, next to everything else in that chat window.

thought through

A greeting lands differently when something turns up to deliver it. So the message became a small character that walks onto your friend's desktop.

built

A desktop app for Mac and Windows. Characters act out greetings, Sparks fill the screen for five seconds, Puchu drafts messages but never sends them, and the Couch keeps a film in step for everyone watching.

now

Public, with downloads at kuchupuchu.me.

Not claimed here. How many people use it, or what it does for a friendship.
