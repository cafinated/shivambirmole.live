Hospital operations

Schedules, maintenance and money at my family's hospital, and the systems I built around them.

Where
My family's hospital
My part
Interviews, redesign, website
Stage
In place

What I noticed

My family works in healthcare, and I have spent time inside our hospital’s daily running. Three problems kept coming up. Staff schedules clashed. Maintenance was not tracked consistently. The financial systems had gaps, so it was hard to see where things stood.

None of these is a medical problem. They are the ordinary operating problems of a place where many people share a small building and the work cannot stop.

How I thought it through

I started by asking. I interviewed patients and staff before changing anything, because the people doing the work already know where it breaks.

Then I took the problems one at a time, starting with the schedule, since a clash there is felt by everyone on the same day.

What I changed

I redesigned the duty schedule and reworked how patients move between steps and how staff hand over to each other. I built a system around the scheduling, maintenance and finance problems. I also built the hospital’s website.

This work is separate from MedDraft, which came later and deals with documents.

Where it stands

The changes are in place. I am not publishing numbers on this page until I can say exactly how they were measured.

What is known, and what is not.

What I did

  • Interviewed patients and staff.
  • Redesigned the duty schedule.
  • Reworked patient flow and handoffs.
  • Built the hospital's website.

What changed

  • A duty schedule built to avoid overlaps.
  • Clearer handoffs between steps of a patient's visit.
  • A website for the hospital.

What has been measured

  • Nothing I can publish yet.

What I do not know yet

  • How scheduling conflicts and waiting times changed, measured properly.
  • What the maintenance and finance tracking should become.
