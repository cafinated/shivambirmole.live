Four years of data, a family hospital, and now products.

I'm Shivam. I think like a founder: I look for a problem worth fixing, and then I build until there is something people can use. I'm from a family that works in healthcare, which is where I first saw how much of a place runs on systems nobody designed.

I studied computer engineering at NMIMS in Mumbai, then spent four years at TresVista in Pune as a data engineer. The job was to take data that arrived messy, from vendors, banks and fund managers, and turn it into something a person could rely on to make a decision.

That habit carried into what I build now. MedDraft takes a doctor's spoken notes and gives them a structure a doctor can check. MU Central takes what scrolls past in a campus group chat and gives it a place. The data work and the products are the same instinct, pointed at different problems.

In college I chaired the NM Debate Society. Debate is where I learned to lay a problem out in columns before arguing about it, which is why this site looks the way it does.

I'm now in the Post Graduate Programme in Technology & Business Management at Masters' Union, and I am still building.
