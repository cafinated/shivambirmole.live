## What it was

In 2018 I finished Class XII and opened a café in Kankavli. I paid for it myself and ran it myself: the operations, the money, the marketing, the menu and the counter.

It ran for a few months.

## The menu

The chalkboard above is the real one. Sandwiches, Maggi four ways, shakes, pancakes, and something called a Maggizza.

## Why it is on this site

It was the first thing I started. Everything else here came after it: the degree, four years of data work, the products. The menu is what is left of it.
