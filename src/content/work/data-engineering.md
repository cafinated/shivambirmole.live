## Investment data

Analysts evaluating a deal need data from several vendors to agree with each other. I built the PySpark and Airflow pipelines that brought vendor data in, and the Databricks workflows that ingested and transformed it. I also benchmarked data quality, so people knew how far to trust a number before they used it.

## Fraud and marketing analytics

A fraud team needs to see a pattern early, and a marketing team needs to know whether a campaign worked. I built Power BI dashboards for both, automated early-stage fraud-detection workflows, analysed campaigns and surveys, and presented the recommendations to stakeholders.

## Fund performance

Fund reporting depends on numbers collected from many people in many formats. I built data-collection templates wired into SQL Server, the backend behind them, and Power BI dashboards on projections, shortfalls and performance, using Python, Azure and SQL.

## The mentorship programme

Inside the company, I led a project to run and measure the mentorship programme with less manual work. Python sends the emails, collects the responses and builds the PowerPoint reports, which gave the programme data to improve from.

## Shining Star Award

I received the Shining Star Award for managing client interactions and leading the team through a demanding project under high client expectations.
